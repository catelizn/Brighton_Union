# bu-mafias

Preset criminal organizations: Italian, Russian, Mexican and Japanese mafia.
Eight ranks (0–7), join only through the faction NPC. Mafias fight for control
over businesses: hold the point at the business for 60 seconds while staying
close, a rival mafia member nearby cancels the war. Every controlled business
pays its `value` into the mafia treasury on the hourly Payday (at :00) — more
profitable businesses pay more. `/mafiamoney` shows the treasury, `/mwithdraw`
withdraws (godfather only), `/quitmafia` leaves the family.
