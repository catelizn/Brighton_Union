# bu-news — Weazel News

Paid classified ads broadcast to the whole server.

## How it works

- The tablet app has a feed and a submission form. An ad costs **$500** from the bank account, with a 60-second cooldown and a 3–100 character limit.
- Once paid, the ad is posted to the **global chat** in orange with the author's name and stored in `bu_ads` (top 50 cached in memory) for the tablet feed.
- This is the intended channel for selling houses and vehicles player-to-player, alongside the marketplace and the auction house.
