# bu-gangs

Preset criminal gangs from the GTA:SA tradition: The Families, Ballas, Vagos,
Bloods, Marabunta. Join only through the gang NPC on the block (`[E]`), ranks
0–4 (rookie → boss). The ghetto is split into 10 capture squares: hold a square
for 60 seconds while staying inside and without rival gang members present.
Controlling 100% of the squares makes your gang the district leader with an
hourly bonus. `Config.QuitCommand` leaves the gang.

Player-made organizations are families — see `bu-families`.
